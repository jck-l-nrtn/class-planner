Setup

Set up your planner

Finishing will replace your current course list. The old files are kept as backups.

1 Canvas

  1. Open your Canvas settings (opens in a new tab).
  2. Click + New Access Token, set the expiration date as late as it allows, then Generate Token.
  3. Copy the token and paste it here.

Canvas tokens expire, often after 30 days. The planner warns you a week ahead.

The token lets the planner read your Canvas courses as you. It is saved in a file in the planner's folder that only your Mac account can read, and it is sent only to Canvas. You can delete it in your Canvas settings at any time.

Back to the planner

2 LearningSuite

LearningSuite has no tokens, so the planner uses its own separate browser window (not Chrome or Safari) to sign in. If you save your password in that window, it is stored in the planner's folder on this Mac, so the planner can sign in again later without you. Anyone who can use your Mac account could use that saved sign-in; deleting the planner's folder removes it. The planner itself never reads your password.

Later, whenever grades are checked, that window opens for a few seconds, signs in with the saved password and closes. That is the planner, not something else.

This opens a separate window for LearningSuite. It closes by itself when you are done.

A separate LearningSuite window just opened. If you don't see it, look behind this one. In that window:

  1. Sign in with your Net ID.
  2. Approve the Duo request and tick Remember this device.
  3. When the browser offers to save your password, click Save. That lets the planner refresh by itself later.

The window closes by itself once you click Save. If your password was saved before, it closes as soon as you are signed in.

3 Your AI

The daily plan and the time estimates are written by an AI running on your own account. Choose one you already have, or one that is free for you.

Assignment titles and descriptions, and how much free time you have, are sent to the one you choose. Your Canvas token and passwords are not.
